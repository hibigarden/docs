# Keyboard sounds

Enable **keyBeats** under **Settings → Addons**, then open its settings to choose a keyboard sound and adjust the volume. Use **Mute** or the keyboard-sound toolbar button to silence it. Hiding the toolbar does not mute the sounds.

Sounds work in both formatted text and source views. keyBeats listens only to typing inside Hibi, and its audio stays on your computer.

Audio prepares in the background after you enable keyBeats, so sounds may not be ready immediately. Preparation does not enumerate devices or ask for microphone or camera access. Output stays suspended until playback is needed and suspends again when muted, at zero volume, when focus leaves the editor or window, when the window is hidden, or after ten seconds without input. Returning to the editor resumes output when you type.
